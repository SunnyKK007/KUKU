### What the law says (only verified sources)
- **Rent and deposit rules are set by each state.** There is no single national law on security deposits. Tenancies are governed by each state's own rental law, plus what your rent agreement says. If your landlord keeps the deposit without a valid reason, it is usually treated as a breach of your agreement. [TENANCY-STATE]
- **Breaking an agreement makes the other side liable to compensate you.** When someone breaks an agreement (like a rent agreement that says the deposit is refundable, or a job offer that fixes your salary), the person who suffers can claim compensation for the loss that naturally follows from the breach. (Section 73 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872) [CONTRACT-BREACH]
- **Model Tenancy Act: a template some states have followed.** The Model Tenancy Act, 2021 is a model law the Centre sent to states in June 2021, for future tenancies. It caps the deposit for a home at two months' rent and says the landlord refunds it when taking back the empty flat, after valid deductions. It applies only where your state has adopted it. As of July 2022, the Centre reported that Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh and Assam had revised their tenancy laws on its lines. (the Model Tenancy Act, 2021) [MTA]
