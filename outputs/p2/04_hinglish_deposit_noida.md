### What the law says (only verified sources)
- **Rent and deposit rules are set by each state.** There is no single national law on security deposits. Tenancies are governed by each state's own rental law, plus what your rent agreement says. If your landlord keeps the deposit without a valid reason, it is usually treated as a breach of your agreement. [TENANCY-STATE]
- **Breaking an agreement makes the other side liable to compensate you.** When someone breaks an agreement (like a rent agreement that says the deposit is refundable, or a job offer that fixes your salary), the person who suffers can claim compensation for the loss that naturally follows from the breach. (Section 73 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872) [CONTRACT-BREACH]
- **Model Tenancy Act: a template some states have followed.** The Model Tenancy Act, 2021 is a model law the Centre sent to states in June 2021, for future tenancies. It caps the deposit for a home at two months' rent and says the landlord refunds it when taking back the empty flat, after valid deductions. It applies only where your state has adopted it. As of July 2022, the Centre reported that Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh and Assam had revised their tenancy laws on its lines. (the Model Tenancy Act, 2021) [MTA]
- **You may be entitled to a free lawyer.** Free legal services are available to, among others: women and children, members of Scheduled Castes or Scheduled Tribes, persons with disability, industrial workmen, people in custody, victims of trafficking or disasters, and people below the income limit set by the state. Contact your District Legal Services Authority. (Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987) [LEGAL-AID]
- You said the house is in **Uttar Pradesh**. The Centre reported Uttar Pradesh revised its tenancy law on the lines of the Model Tenancy Act. Your state's own Act may set up a Rent Authority and limits on deposits, but I do not hold its exact text. Check it or ask legal aid. [MTA]
- For reference, the model law caps a home deposit at two months' rent (Rs 24,000 for you). Whether your state copied this cap, and whether it applies to your tenancy's start date, needs checking. [MTA]

### Your options, step by step
1. **Ask in writing (today).** Send a short WhatsApp/email: the amount, the date you vacated, and a date to pay by. Ask for an itemised bill for any deduction (for example 'painting') and which clause of the agreement allows it.
2. **Send a legal notice (draft ready in 'Draft notice').** Give 15 days. Send by Speed Post or Registered Post with acknowledgement, keep the receipt, and also send it by email/WhatsApp. You can send it yourself or through a lawyer.
3. **Talk / settle.** Many landlords pay after a formal notice. If they offer part, decide whether a quick settlement is worth more to you than the full amount later. Get any settlement in writing.
4. **Rent Authority / Rent Court (your state's tenancy Act).** Your state revised its tenancy law on Model Tenancy Act lines, so there may be a Rent Authority for disputes. Ask the District Collector's office or legal aid which office handles it. [MTA]
5. **Free help any time:** NALSA legal aid helpline 15100. [NALSA-15100]
