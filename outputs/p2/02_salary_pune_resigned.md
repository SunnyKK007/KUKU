### What the law says (only verified sources)
- **The Code on Wages is in force from 21 Nov 2025.** The Code on Wages, 2019 came into force on 21 Nov 2025 and repealed the old Payment of Wages Act, 1936. State rules under the Code are at different stages, so the exact office and forms can differ by state. (Section 69 of the Code on Wages, 2019; the Payment of Wages Act, 1936) [COW-IN-FORCE]
- **Salary must be paid by the 7th; final dues within 2 working days of leaving.** For monthly-paid staff, wages must be paid before the 7th of the next month. If you resigned, were removed, dismissed or retrenched, or the business closed, the wages due must be paid within two working days. (Section 17 of the Code on Wages, 2019) [COW-TIMELINE]
- **Claim unpaid wages before the government's Wage Authority, within 3 years.** The government appoints an authority (a Gazetted Officer) to hear wage claims. You, your registered trade union, or the Inspector-cum-Facilitator can file. File within three years (later only with sufficient cause). The authority can also order compensation of up to ten times the claim, and should try to decide within three months. (Section 45 of the Code on Wages, 2019) [COW-CLAIM]
- **Breaking an agreement makes the other side liable to compensate you.** When someone breaks an agreement (like a rent agreement that says the deposit is refundable, or a job offer that fixes your salary), the person who suffers can claim compensation for the loss that naturally follows from the breach. (Section 73 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872) [CONTRACT-BREACH]
- **You may be entitled to a free lawyer.** Free legal services are available to, among others: women and children, members of Scheduled Castes or Scheduled Tribes, persons with disability, industrial workmen, people in custody, victims of trafficking or disasters, and people below the income limit set by the state. Contact your District Legal Services Authority. (Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987) [LEGAL-AID]
